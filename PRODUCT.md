# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Business owners and operating teams evaluating Corevix Solutions for website development, website repair, AI automation, SaaS products, and smart dashboards. They need clear evidence of relevant work, practical capabilities, and trustworthy delivery before starting a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Present Corevix Solutions as a capable digital systems partner and convert qualified visitors into project enquiries or appointments. Success means visitors can quickly understand the offer, inspect real work, and choose a clear next step.

## Brand Personality

Technical, confident, restrained. The voice is direct and practical, with enough detail to establish expertise without inflated claims or unnecessary jargon.

## Anti-references

Avoid glossy generic SaaS styling, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, hype-heavy copy, repetitive card grids, and visual effects that compete with project evidence.

## Design Principles

1. Show real work before making broad claims.
2. Make technical capability understandable to business decision-makers.
3. Use disciplined hierarchy and spacing to communicate confidence.
4. Keep every call to action specific and low-friction.
5. Preserve the established Corevix visual language across new content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain readable contrast, visible focus states, semantic structure, descriptive alternative text, keyboard access, and reduced-motion support.
